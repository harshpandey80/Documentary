# Primary Sources & Historical Records: History of England Under 60 Seconds

## Primary References & Declassified Historical Records

### 1. Roman Britannia & Conquest (43 AD)
- **Source**: Tacitus, *Agricola* & *Annals*; Cassius Dio, *Roman History* Book LX.
- **Reference URL**: https://www.britannica.com/place/Roman-Britain
- **Key Metrics**: Roman Emperor Claudius launched the invasion of Britain in 43 AD, establishing Britannia as an imperial province for nearly 400 years.

### 2. Anglo-Saxon Heptarchy & 7 Kingdoms
- **Source**: Bede, *Historia ecclesiastica gentis Anglorum* (Ecclesiastical History of the English People, 731 AD).
- **Reference URL**: https://www.britannica.com/topic/heptarchy
- **Key Metrics**: 7 major Anglo-Saxon kingdoms (Northumbria, Mercia, East Anglia, Essex, Kent, Sussex, Wessex). Over 2000 years of continuous development.

### 3. The Norman Conquest (1066) & Domesday Book (1086)
- **Source**: *The Anglo-Saxon Chronicle*; The Bayeux Tapestry (Musée de la Tapisserie de Bayeux); The National Archives UK (Domesday Book, E 31/2).
- **Reference URL**: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/domesday/
- **Key Metrics**: 1066 Battle of Hastings; King Harold struck down; William the Conqueror crowned at Westminster Abbey.

### 4. Magna Carta & Constitutional Law (1215)
- **Source**: British Library Cotton MS Augustus II.106; *Statutes of the Realm*.
- **Reference URL**: https://www.bl.uk/magna-carta
- **Key Metrics**: Sealed at Runnymede in June 1215, establishing fundamental rights and limiting the absolute power of the monarch.

### 5. James Watt Steam Engine Patent (1769)
- **Source**: UK Intellectual Property Office Patent No. 913 (1769) "A New Invented Method of Lessening the Consumption of Steam and Fuel in Fire Engines".
- **Reference URL**: https://www.nms.ac.uk/explore-our-collections/stories/science-and-technology/james-watt/
- **Key Metrics**: 1769 patent ignited the Industrial Revolution, multiplying global manufacturing power.

### 6. Peak British Empire Territorial & Demographic Reach
- **Source**: *The Cambridge History of the British Empire*; Maddison Project Database; Encyclopædia Britannica.
- **Reference URL**: https://www.britannica.com/place/British-Empire
- **Key Metrics**:
  - Land Area: 35500000 sq km (35.5 million km², the largest empire in human history).
  - Global Proportion: 24% of the Earth's total land area.
  - Population: 412000000 people (412 million subjects, one-fifth of humanity).

### 7. World War II & Decolonization (1940, 1945)
- **Source**: Imperial War Museums (IWM), UK National Archives CAB 65/66 (War Cabinet Minutes).
- **Reference URLs**:
  - https://www.iwm.org.uk/history/the-blitz-around-britain (The Blitz, 1940)
  - https://www.iwm.org.uk/history/what-was-ve-day (VE Day, 1945)
- **Key Metrics**: The 1940 Blitz aerial campaign; 1945 victory leaving Britain financially exhausted, triggering the wave of independence.
