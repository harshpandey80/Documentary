# Source 06: High-Resolution Multibeam Mapping of the Challenger Deep, Mariana Trench

- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-13-3375-2021
- **Authors**: Cassie Bongiovanni, Heather A. Stewart, Jamieson A. Jamieson
- **Journal**: Earth System Science Data (ESSD), Vol. 13, pp. 3375–3393
- **Date**: July 2021
- **Institution**: Caladan Oceanic / British Geological Survey / Five Deeps Expedition

### Saved Excerpt
> In June 2020, repeated full-ocean-depth submersible dives with the Deep Submergence Vehicle (DSV) Limiting Factor coupled with high-resolution hull-mounted Kongsberg EM124 multibeam bathymetry mapped the three sub-basins of Challenger Deep (Eastern, Central, and Western). Using calibrated in situ conductivity-temperature-depth (CTD) sensors and pressure transponders, the deepest point was surveyed at 10,935 ± 6 meters in the Eastern Basin (11°19.9′ N, 142°12.0′ E).

### Key Verified Metrics
- **Measured Submersible Depth**: 10,935 meters (±6 m)
- **Primary Survey Platform**: DSV Limiting Factor (Triton 36000/2) + Kongsberg EM124
- **Date of Survey**: June 2020
- **Basins Surveyed**: Eastern, Central, and Western
