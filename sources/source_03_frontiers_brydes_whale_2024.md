# Source 03: Bryde's Whales Produce the Western Pacific Biotwang

- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2024.1394697
- **Authors**: Ann N. Allen, et al.
- **Journal**: Frontiers in Marine Science, Vol. 11
- **Date**: April 2024
- **Institution**: NOAA Pacific Islands Fisheries Science Center / Google AI for Nature

### Saved Excerpt
> Combining machine learning classification models with concurrent visual sightings and acoustic array tracking in the Mariana Archipelago, researchers conclusively linked the enigmatic 'Biotwang' sound to Bryde's whales (Balaenoptera edeni). The unique metallic frequency modulation, reaching up to 8 kHz, represents a specialized regional dialect produced by a discrete population in the western North Pacific, settling an eight-year biological mystery.
