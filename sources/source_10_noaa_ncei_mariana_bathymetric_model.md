# Source 10: NOAA NCEI Bathymetry of the Mariana Trench

- **URL**: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/products/bathymetry-data
- **Authors**: NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information (NCEI)
- **Date**: 2021
- **Institution**: NOAA Marine Geology and Geophysics Division

### Saved Excerpt
> The Mariana Trench forms an arcuate crescent roughly 2,550 kilometers (1,580 miles) long with an average width of 69 kilometers (43 miles). The trench reaches its maximum depth at the southern extremity in the Challenger Deep, a small en-echelon trough roughly 11 km wide and 40 km long. Multibeam sounding grids assembled by NOAA compile soundings from the 2010 US Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping expedition and subsequent GEBCO updates.

### Key Verified Metrics
- **Trench Total Arc Length**: 2,550 kilometers (1,580 miles)
- **Average Trench Width**: 69 kilometers (43 miles)
- **Challenger Deep Trough Dimensions**: ~11 km wide by 40 km long
- **Geographic Orientation**: Crescent curving northeast to southwest in western Pacific
