# Source 11: Five Deeps Expedition - DSV Limiting Factor Challenger Deep Dives (2019)

- **URL**: https://fivedeeps.com/pacific-ocean/
- **Authors**: Victor Vescovo, Triton Submarines LLC, Caladan Oceanic
- **Date**: May 2019
- **Institution**: Caladan Oceanic / DNV GL Maritime Certification

### Saved Excerpt
> In April and May 2019, explorer Victor Vescovo completed four manned descents to the floor of the Challenger Deep aboard DSV Limiting Factor (a commercially certified Triton 36000/2 submersible). Across these dives, the submersible's dual redundant pressure transponders recorded bottom contact at 10,928 meters (35,853 ft), with a subsequent dive measuring 10,927 m. The vehicle's 90mm thick titanium pressure sphere successfully carried two occupants during each mission and spent up to four hours on the seafloor conducting mapping passes and biological sampling.

### Key Verified Metrics
- **Dive Dates**: April 28 – May 7, 2019 (4 dives total)
- **Measured Depth**: 10,928 meters (35,853 ft)
- **Hull Material & Thickness**: Grade 23 Ti-6Al-4V ELI titanium, 90 millimeters (3.54 in)
- **Crew Capacity**: 2 persons
- **Bottom Time**: Up to 4 hours per dive
- **Certification Standard**: DNV GL certified for unlimited full-ocean depth operations
