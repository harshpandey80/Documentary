# Source 13: HMS Challenger II Echo Sounding Survey (1951)

- **URL**: https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-hydrographic-office
- **Authors**: Admiralty Hydrographic Department / Commander G.S. Ritchie, RN
- **Date**: October 1951
- **Institution**: United Kingdom Hydrographic Office (UKHO)

### Saved Excerpt
> In October 1951, the Royal Navy survey vessel HMS Challenger (under Commander Ritchie) conducted echo-sounding lines across the southern Marianas. Using taut-wire sounding gear and a high-precision quartz echo-sounder, the crew registered an uncorrected depth of 5,960 fathoms. Subsequent temperature and salinity sound-velocity corrections adjusted the figure to 5,960 fathoms (10,900 meters / 35,760 feet) at 11°19′ N, 142°11′ E, giving this extreme depression the name 'Challenger Deep'.
