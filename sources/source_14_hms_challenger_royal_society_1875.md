# Source 14: HMS Challenger Expedition Initial Sounding (1875)

- **URL**: https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rstl.1876.0007
- **Authors**: Captain George S. Nares, RN, and C. Wyville Thomson
- **Journal**: Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society of London
- **Date**: March 1875
- **Institution**: The Royal Society of London

### Saved Excerpt
> On 23 March 1875, at Station 225 southwest of Guam (11°24′ N, 143°16′ E), the Challenger Expedition deployed sounding gear with a Baillie rod and hemp line weighted with hundreds of pounds of iron sinkers. The sounding wire ran out to 4,475 fathoms (8,184 meters / 26,850 feet), marking the first recorded sounding of the trench system. Bottom samples recovered consisted of red clay with radiolarian tests, establishing that life and sedimentation persist into extreme oceanic troughs.
