# Source 05: So, How Deep Is the Mariana Trench?

- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1080/01490419.2013.837849
- **Authors**: James V. Gardner, Andrew A. Armstrong, Brian R. Calder, John Beaudoin
- **Journal**: Marine Geodesy, Vol. 37, Issue 1, pp. 1–13
- **Date**: January 2014
- **Institution**: Center for Coastal and Ocean Mapping / Joint Hydrographic Center, University of New Hampshire & NOAA

### Saved Excerpt
> A multibeam echo-sounding survey of the entire Mariana Trench was conducted using a hull-mounted Kongsberg EM122 system (12 kHz). Ray-tracing sound velocity profiles were integrated across the water column to depth. The absolute deepest point within the Challenger Deep western basin was determined to be 10,984 ± 25 meters (36,037 ft) at 95% confidence level, positioned at 11°22.4′ N, 142°35.5′ E.

### Key Verified Metrics

- **Confidence Interval**: 95%
