# Source 08: Deepsea Challenge Expedition - Solo Dive to Challenger Deep (2012)

- **URL**: https://www.nationalgeographic.com/expeditions/deepsea-challenge/
- **Authors**: James Cameron, National Geographic Society Expedition Team
- **Date**: March 2012
- **Institution**: National Geographic Society / Earthship Productions

### Saved Excerpt
> On 26 March 2012, filmmaker and explorer James Cameron piloted the Deepsea Challenger submersible to the seafloor of the Challenger Deep, completing the first solo dive to the trench. The vertically oriented submersible descended 10,908 meters (35,787 ft) in 2 hours and 36 minutes. The pilot sphere was fabricated from high-strength forged steel with an interior diameter of 43 inches (109 cm). Cameron remained on the bottom for 2 hours and 34 minutes before hydraulic fluid line failures forced an early ascent.
