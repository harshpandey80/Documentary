# Source 04: Project Nekton - Operation of Bathyscaphe Trieste (1960)

- **URL**: https://www.history.navy.mil/browse-by-topic/exploration-and-scientific-research/project-nekton.html
- **Authors**: Office of Naval Research (ONR) / U.S. Naval History and Heritage Command
- **Date**: January 1960 (Official Naval Records)
- **Institution**: United States Navy, Bureau of Ships

### Saved Excerpt
> On 23 January 1960, the United States Navy bathyscaphe Trieste achieved the first manned descent to the floor of the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench. Piloted by Jacques Piccard and Lieutenant Don Walsh, USN, the vessel reached an indicated depth of 37,800 feet, later corrected by the Navy Hydrographic Office to 35,800 feet (10,911 meters). The descent took 4 hours and 47 minutes under a surface water temperature of 84°F and seafloor temperature of 37.4°F (3.0°C). Outer Plexiglas viewing ports sustained structural surface cracking under 16,880 pounds per square inch of ambient water pressure, but the 5-inch thick Krupp forged steel sphere remained structurally intact.
